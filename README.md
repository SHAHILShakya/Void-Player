## About

**VOID PLAYER** is an Android music player built around a simple idea: your local music library should feel personal, elegant, and effortless to use.

MIRAGE v3 continues that direction with a visual-first experience, customizable listening surfaces, and tools that make a local music collection feel like your own space.

> **Your music. Your identity. Your experience.**

## Features

- **Offline-first listening** — enjoy music from your local device library.
- **Personalized player surfaces** — choose between the existing Classic Vinyl and Squaricle Liquid Glass artwork styles.
- **Visual customization** — tailor the look and feel through the app's appearance settings.
- **Song personalization** — customize supported song details and personal comments.
- **Listening utilities** — use features such as the sleep timer and other built-in player options.
- **Dynamic visual identity** — an interface designed to complement the currently displayed music artwork.

Feature availability may vary by build. Refer to the release notes for the exact contents of each version.

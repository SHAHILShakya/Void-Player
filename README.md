## ◈ The idea

Music was never just about pressing play.

It is the artwork you remember, the song you return to, the atmosphere you build around a moment, and the small details that make a collection feel unmistakably yours.

**VOID PLAYER** is an offline-first Android music player shaped around that thought. It brings your local music collection into an artwork-focused listening environment, with visual styles and personalization that let the experience feel more like your own.

No streaming-first identity. No unnecessary social layer. Just your music, presented with intention.

## ✦ At a glance

| Experience | What it brings |
|---|---|
| Local music library | Browse and play supported audio stored on your device |

## ◈ The experience

### Your music, at the centre

VOID PLAYER is designed around supported audio stored on your device. The library and playback experience keep your own collection at the heart of the app.

### Two artwork expressions

**Classic Vinyl**  
A record-inspired circular presentation that gives album artwork a different visual character, with vinyl-inspired rings and playback-associated motion.

**Squaricle Liquid Glass**  
A rounded-square artwork presentation with a translucent, glass-inspired visual identity. A softer geometric counterpart to the circular Vinyl style.

### A listening space you can shape

The app includes appearance and experience customization, with visual concepts spanning static and live backgrounds, artwork styles, progress-line presentation, and artwork-aware accent-color behavior. Exact options may vary by build.

**Static background concepts**  
Void Silk · Obsidian Glass · Aurora Void · Liquid Metal · Cosmic Void

**Live background concepts**  
Void Particles · Void Atmosphere · Neural Threads · Pulse Grid · Live Heart

### Personal details belong to the listener

The Customize Song experience supports individual-track details such as song title, artist name, personal comments, and an artwork customization workflow.

### Tools for the moment

- **Sleep Timer** — set a listening time limit.
- **Equalizer** — access audio adjustment controls.
- **VOID Analytics** — explore the app's music-related insights area.
- **VOID Studio** — open a dedicated creative workspace.

## ◈ Product direction

VOID PLAYER is being developed as a personal music environment, not merely an audio-file browser.

The ongoing direction is to keep improving:

- Local music-library navigation.
- Artwork and player presentation.
- Song-level personalization.
- Appearance and listening preferences.
- Reliability, performance, and everyday usability.

Ideas under consideration are not promises of availability. A feature should be treated as released only when it is implemented, tested, and included in a published build.

## ◈ Privacy & security

A few important rules for keeping the project and its users safe:

- Never commit API keys, access tokens, passwords, or private credentials.
- Never commit signing keystores or keystore passwords.
- Keep machine-specific configuration out of version control.
- Review screenshots, logs, and sample data for personal information.
- Do not publish private user data in issues or discussions.
- Report security-sensitive vulnerabilities privately rather than posting exploitable details publicly.
